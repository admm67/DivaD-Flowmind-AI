# Manufacturing Application Guide

## Define the system
Capture product family and routing, demand/mix, shift calendar, capacity and
actual output by operation, WIP/queues, scrap/rework/first-pass yield, changeover,
downtime, shortages, and mandatory safety/quality/environmental controls.

## Defect diagnosis
1. Define defect operationally and confirm measurement method.
2. Stratify by product, machine, tool/cavity, shift, material lot, operator
   qualification, recipe, and time.
3. Verify measurement-system suitability.
4. Check process history and changes.
5. Build causal hypotheses and discriminating tests.
6. Contain risk through approved procedures.
7. Verify corrective action with sustained data and recurrence checks.

## Flow diagnosis
Determine whether the constraint is machine, labor/skill, material, quality
release, maintenance policy, planning rule, or market demand. Examine queues
and lost production at the candidate constraint.

## Improvement charter
Problem and baseline; scope; customer impact; target/time horizon; owner/team;
risks and mandatory controls; analysis and pilot plan; control plan and handover.

Never bypass interlocks, guards, permits, lockout procedures, approved recipes,
process limits, or quality release controls. Follow site emergency procedures.
