# Decision Quality Review

## Frame
What decision, by when, by whom, against which system goal? Is it reversible?

## Alternatives
Include status quo and materially different options. Avoid false binaries.
Identify feasibility constraints.

## Information
Separate facts, estimates, assumptions, and unknowns. Identify uncertainties
that could reverse the choice. Seek the cheapest useful test or pilot. Use
ranges and scenarios rather than false precision.

## Consequences
Assess short/long term, system/local effects, financial/non-financial outcomes,
safety, quality, compliance, customers, people, reputation, and displaced
constraints.

## Commitment
Record decision owner, rationale, dissent, reconsideration trigger, first
actions/resources, leading indicators, and review date.
