# Example: recurring manufacturing defect

**Illustrative only — not a real plant diagnosis.**

Do not immediately prescribe a parameter adjustment. Define the defect and
baseline rate; stratify by product, machine, tool/cavity, shift, material lot,
and time; check inspection consistency; build competing causal hypotheses;
identify tests that distinguish them; maintain approved containment; pilot a
verified change; and check sustained performance and recurrence.

A completed analysis must show evidence for the selected cause and explain
how recurrence will be monitored.
